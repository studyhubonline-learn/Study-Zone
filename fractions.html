What are fractions? Is probably what we are all thinking

>I will admit, fractions are pretty annoying at first. But once you understand them, they are much easier than they look!

A fraction shows how many equal parts of a whole you have. When a whole is divided into equal parts, a fraction tells you how many of those parts are being used.
